## Brand & Style

This design system translates the heavy industry and tactile reality of an agricultural processing plant in the Democratic Republic of the Congo into an editorial, documentary-style digital interface. It departs entirely from modern sterile SaaS conventions, instead adopting an aesthetic grounded in photojournalistic archives, industrial plant signage, burlap sacks, mechanical machinery, and rich arable earth.

The visual style is characterized as **Tactile Industrial Editorial**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep soil, roasted grains, hot cold-press extraction, raw steel, heavy craft paper, and intense daylight.
- **Narrative tone:** Authentic, grounded, disciplined, and dignified. It communicates operational excellence, provenance, and the direct link between local farming communities and industrial-scale transformation (groundnut paste, virgin oils, cassava flour).
- **Visual anchors:** Asymmetrical architectural grid structures, sharp structural lines, micro-technical metadata stampings (batch dates, extraction metrics, origins), and tactile material contrast between deep earthen solids and raw unbleached fiber backings.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from raw agricultural commodities, mechanical pressings, and Congolese soil.

- **Primary Earth Solid (`#241B14`):** An ultra-deep, warm organic brown-black evoking humus-rich volcanic and alluvial soils, machine cast iron, and high-contrast typography. Used for critical hierarchy, headlines, structural rules, and deep contrast container blocks.
- **Secondary Roasted Shell (`#8C5632` with accent `#B87333`):** Evokes roasted groundnut shells, fired brick kilns, and weathered wood pallets. Anchors subheaders, structural framing, badges, and secondary callouts.
- **Tertiary Pressed Amber (`#D99126` with accent `#EAA638`):** The golden glow of virgin groundnut oil and processed grain oils. Used selectively for live status badges, highlights, interactive focal points, and technical readouts.
- **Neutral Ivory & Fiber Bone (`#FBF8F2` canvas, `#F3ECE1` surface):** The foundational substrate reminiscent of raw archival documentation paper, sacks, and bleached starch. Replaces sterile digital white across all surfaces.
- **Botanical Foliage (`#2A442E`):** A deep vegetative canopy green used as an institutional and ecological accent for sustainability metrics, certified lot approvals, and agricultural field telemetry.

Avoid glowing neons, artificial gradients, cold blues, and translucent purple drop-shadows. Tinted warm mid-tones and structural opaque layering maintain the earthy, unvarnished tonality.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the sturdy weight of an industrial slab-inflected serif with the clean, utilitarian legibility of an engineered grotesque sans-serif.

- **Headlines (`Domine`):** Chosen for its structural slab presence, strong brackets, and robust serifs that evoke printed industrial ledger books, heritage agricultural manifests, and heavy physical press machinery. Display and headline levels command weight and presence without delicate fragility.
- **Body and Technical Documentation (`Work Sans`):** Neutral, architectural, and highly legible even in harsh field conditions or low-contrast ambient plant environments. It handles production charts, batch numbers, dispatch records, and long narrative reporting cleanly.
- **Technical Stampings (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase Work Sans with widened tracking (`0.08em`) to mirror stamped shipping stencils, harvest batch lot numbers, and machine specifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured editorial grid reflecting photojournalistic photo-essays and industrial production schedules. 

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column asymmetrical grid with wide `3rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content frequently pairs wide 7-column or 8-column media/documentary assets with 4-column or 5-column technical metrics or narrative logs.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding.
- **Rhythm & Composition:** Spacing relies on stark deliberate pauses (`space-2xl`) between plant operations or chapters, countered by tight data-density (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) inside technical inspection panels and dispatch logs.
- **Layout Division:** Instead of floating whitespace cards, content groups are separated by crisp, deliberate 1px linear rules (`#241B14` at low opacity, or solid `#8C5632`), reinforcing physical ledger lines and print registration marks.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating digital cards, diffused colored shadows, and synthetic blurs. Depth is tactile, layered, and planar:

- **Flat Ledger Stacking:** Surface hierarchy is achieved through opaque color blocking: `#FBF8F2` (base field canvas), transitioning to `#F3ECE1` (delineated technical panel), and culminating in inverted deep soil `#241B14` (command consoles, batch certificates, hero blocks).
- **Physical Borders & Separation:** Depth relies on crisp `1px` structural borders (`#241B14` with an opacity of 15% to 30%, or `#8C5632` at 40%). High-priority alert modules or hero frames use solid, unbroken `2px` borders in `#241B14`.
- **Mechanical Offset Shadow (Sparse):** When interactive floating affordances are strictly required (e.g., floating dispatch action button or hovering document drawer), use a hard, mechanical 2px to 3px solid offset shadow (`#241B14` at 100% opacity, 0px blur) rather than diffused radial falloffs, mimicking industrial metal plate overlays and stamped paper.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**:

- Every button, input field, status badge, photograph frame, and modal container utilizes `0px` border-radius.
- Crisp 90-degree corners align with physical industrial machinery, packing crates, cut paper logs, and agricultural silo schematics.
- Status indicators and visual stamp tags employ sharp rectangular cutouts or chamfered utility badges rather than soft rounded pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Industrial Button:** Sharp rectangular button with a solid `#241B14` background, `#FBF8F2` typography in `label-caps`, padding of `space-sm space-lg`. On hover, the surface shifts to `#8C5632` with zero transition lag or an immediate snap. Active state applies an inner 1px `#FBF8F2` hairline inset.
- **Secondary Shell Button:** Unfilled, 1px solid border in `#241B14`, text in `#241B14`. On hover, fills with `#F3ECE1`.
- **Tertiary Amber Button (Production Actions):** Background of `#D99126`, text in `#241B14` with bold weight (`fontWeight: 600`). Used for operational triggers (e.g., "COMMENCER LE PRESSAGE", "VALIDER LE LOT").

### Chips & Badges
- **Technical Lot Tags:** Sharp-edged rectangular containers with a 1px border in `#241B14` (20% opacity), background `#F3ECE1`, using `label-caps` in `#241B14`.
- **Status Indicators:**
  - *Silo / Qualité Conforme:* Background `#2A442E`, text `#FBF8F2`.
  - *Torréfaction / Alerte:* Background `#D99126`, text `#241B14`.
  - *Arrêt Machine:* Background `#8C5632`, text `#FBF8F2`.

### Cards & Ledger Blocks
- Built on `#F3ECE1` with a top 2px accent rule in `#8C5632` or `#241B14`. 
- No drop-shadows. Internal spacing uses `space-md` or `space-lg`.
- Often displays a two-part construction: a header strip showing uppercase metadata (e.g., "UNITÉ 03 — EXTRACTION D'HUILE"), followed by raw photography or technical yield metrics.

### Input Fields
- Square corners, 1px bottom border or complete 1px perimeter border in `#241B14` (30% opacity), background `#FBF8F2`. 
- Active focus state changes border to 2px solid `#241B14` or `#D99126`, with no outer glow or halo.
- Labels sit persistently in uppercase `label-caps` directly above the field.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Custom square box, 1px solid `#241B14`. When selected, filled solid with `#241B14` containing a sharp, non-rounded vector checkmark in `#FBF8F2`. Radio buttons are rendered as square boxes enclosing a solid inner square block.

### Documentary Imagery & Data Tables
- **Photojournalism Frames:** Images are displayed full-bleed or bordered with a 1px border in `#241B14`. Captions appear underneath in `body-sm` accompanied by a label-caps timestamp and location coordinates (e.g., "USINE SUD — HAUT-KATANGA").
- **Tables:** Styled as heavy ledger sheets. Header rows use `#241B14` with `#FBF8F2` text. Data rows alternate slightly between `#FBF8F2` and `#F3ECE1`, separated by 1px hairline rules.
